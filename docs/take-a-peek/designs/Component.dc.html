Rules, so it never gets tiresome
  • At most once per page, never between artworks in a grid.
  • Only where something can be bought: never on About, Contact, legal pages or works without a print.
  • No pop-ups, sticky bars or sound. The modal opens only when the visitor chooses.
  • The circle loops only while on screen. A still frame with reduced motion.
  • On an artwork page the modal's button orders that work: "Order a print of Magnolia IX".
  • Never inside the checkout steps. The cart is the last place it appears.
