How it works

With or without a passepartout (or as the bare sheet), a limited edition with its pencilled number. The artwork itself is never in the render: it sits, untouched, in the render’s window. Renders run here, in this browser, one work at a time. A work that’s out of date keeps its last render on the site until it’s re-rendered.
