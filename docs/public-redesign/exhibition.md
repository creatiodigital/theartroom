# Public site · Exhibition page (`/exhibitions/{artist}/{exhibition}`)

Approved by the owner 2026-11-11. Designs: `designs/exhibition-desktop.dc.html`, `designs/exhibition-phone.dc.html`
(drawn with *The Beauty of Permanence*, real text and real framed renders; some works left out on the board).
A light variant of today's page: same header, footer, fonts and link style as the other public pages. **No underlines
anywhere.** UI only: data, links, cart and the 3D room entry work as today.

1. **Hero** (layout as today): left column 360 px, right column the featured image 3:2.
   - The artist's name in small caps (12 px, `#555`, letter-spacing .16em), linking to the artist page, no underline.
   - The title in EB Garamond 50 (36 on phone).
   - Under the title, one grey line: *"26 works · 3 series"* (counts from the data; "1 series" singular; the
     series part is left out when the exhibition has no series).
   - The black **Enter 3D room** button with the Phosphor Cube icon, as today.
   - Phone: the image first at full content width, then the text, and the button full width.
2. **About the exhibition**: the same two columns as the hero. On the left a small grey caps label
   *"About the exhibition"*; on the right the **full** text (every paragraph, no "Continue reading"), Lato 17 / 1.75,
   `#444`, max 760 px. Phone: label then the text, 16 / 1.7.
3. **Series** (as today): centered small-caps serif heading between two hairlines, with the number of works under it
   in grey (*"10 works"*). No series index or jump menu.
   - Works in 3 columns (2 on phone), the framed presentation renders on `#f2f2f2`, as today.
   - Caption: the title in EB Garamond italics 19 and the year in grey below. **The artist's name is not repeated**
     on each work.
   - **Order print**, only on works with a print: an outlined button (1 px `#1a1a1a`, 38 px high, Lato 12 caps,
     letter-spacing .12em, the same bag icon as the header cart (Phosphor ShoppingBagOpen, light) 16 px). It sits on the right of the caption on desktop and full
     width under the caption on phone. Same link as today.
4. **Walk through the exhibition** band, background `#f8f8f6`: heading in EB Garamond 36 (28 on phone),
   *"See the exhibition in an interactive virtual space."*, and a second black **Enter 3D room** button on the right
   (full width under the text on phone).
5. **About the artist**: a small grey caps label, then one row in the same style as the Artists page (portrait 3:2,
   name in serif 28, the first lines of the bio, **→ View artist**). Phone: portrait, name, → View artist.
6. The new footer.
