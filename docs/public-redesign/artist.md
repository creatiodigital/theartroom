# Public site · Artist page (`/artists/{artist}`)

Approved by the owner 2026-11-11. Designs: `designs/artist-desktop.dc.html`, `designs/artist-phone.dc.html`
(drawn with Elizabeth Mosler, real bio and renders; some works left out on the board). Same pattern as the
exhibition page (`exhibition.md`). **No underlines anywhere.** UI only: data and links as today.

1. **Hero:** left column 360 px: small grey caps *"Artist"*, the name in EB Garamond 50 (36 on phone), then a grey
   line *"1 exhibition · 26 works"* (counts from the data, singular/plural). Right: the artist's photo, 3:2.
   Phone: photo first at full content width, then the text.
2. **About the artist:** same two columns: grey caps label *"About the artist"* on the left, the **full** bio on the
   right (Lato 17 / 1.75, `#444`, max 760 px). No columns of small text, no "read more".
3. **Exhibitions:** centered serif caps heading between hairlines with the count under it (*"1 exhibition"*), then
   one row per exhibition exactly like the Exhibitions page (`exhibitions.md`), **without the artist's name**
   (the page is about them). Phone: the Exhibitions-page phone row (image at content width, text below).
4. **Works** (today "Featured works"): same heading style with *"26 works"*, renders in 3 columns (2 on phone),
   caption = title in serif italics + year (no artist name), and the **Order print** button on every work that has
   a print (see README, "Order print").
5. The new footer.
