Sizes in cm or inches: a line of its own in the footer

A minor feature, so it lives in the footer only (owner 2026-11-17). Plain text, no dropdown (only two choices): Sizes in cm | in, the chosen unit bold. No switch anywhere else: not in the header, not on the artwork page, not in the print wizard.
One saved choice for the whole site (this browser, cm by default, never set by country). Every size a visitor sees follows it. Editors always see cm.

A line of its own

Its own row, after the legal links and before the ©, with the same hairline as every part of the footer. Not mixed with the links. Same 14 px grey as the legal row; the chosen unit bold.
