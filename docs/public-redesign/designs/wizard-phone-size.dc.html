Print wizard on phones: choosing a free size without a knob (open editions)

Owner 2026-11-17: on phones the slider knobs sit near the screen edge; dragging them starts the system’s swipe-back or moves the page (Safari and Chrome). Open editions stay free: any size in the range, no fixed sizes. None of these has a knob to aim at. Price at the bottom updates live.

A · A ruler you swipe (recommended)

The size is written large; under it a ruler slides under a fixed marker in the centre. Swipe anywhere on the ruler (left = bigger, right = smaller, like a measuring tape): a flick travels far, a slow drag fine-tunes to 0.1 cm. The action happens mid-screen, far from the edge. Beyond the smallest and largest the ruler is pale and springs back. The paper border uses a short ruler (0–10 cm, “None” at 0). “Type a size” opens the fields.
