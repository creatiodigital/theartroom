Artwork page: “Print sizes”, and a clear range for open editions

Today a limited edition with two variants shows two bare size lines under the year, and an open edition a bare range (“30.0 × 20.0 cm to 127.0 × 84.7 cm”), with nothing to say what they are. Each option adds a few words; the sizes stay in the visitor’s unit (cm | in from the footer). Same wording on the artwork details in the 3D room.

O3 · Recommended: smallest, largest, and “or any size in between”

Nothing to guess (owner: “I don’t want to make users guess”): the two ends are named and the last line says the rest. Limited editions use the same label and add one grey line saying it’s a limited edition in two sizes, so the two lines aren’t a mystery either.

O1 · Open edition: one line, “From … to …”

The simplest: the label, then one line. Short, but the visitor still has to work out that any size in between is possible.
O1
John Doe High Res 2020 Print sizesFrom 30.0 × 20.0 cm to 127.0 × 84.7 cm Adox Silvermax 100 film, Leica M6 / Original silver gelatin negative developed by hand

O2 · Open edition: “Choose any size…”

Says plainly that the buyer picks the size. Warmer, but two lines in the column.
O2
John Doe High Res 2020 Print sizesChoose any size, from 30.0 × 20.0 cm up to 127.0 × 84.7 cm Adox Silvermax 100 film, Leica M6 / Original silver gelatin negative developed by hand

O4 · Open edition: a small drawing

Two outlined rectangles, small and large, with their sizes, joined by a line: you can see the range at a glance. The most visual; the rectangles keep the artwork’s proportions.
